  How we find the "Hidden" Contacts (Emails/Phones):
  We don't just look at a profile. Our agents will:
   1. Scrape GitHub Commits: We look at the "hidden" metadata in their code uploads where their real email is often
      stored.
   2. WHOIS Data: If they have a personal website (e.g., sarahdev.com), we pull the registration data for their
      phone/email.
   3. Cross-Platform Matching: If we find them on LinkedIn, we hunt for their Twitter/X or YouTube to find "Contact Me"
      info they left in descriptions.

  Why this is "God-Mode" for Agencies:
  Most agencies spend weeks trying to find a phone number or getting an HR person to look at a resume. Recruit2Result
  does it in minutes. By contacting both the candidate and the HR at the same time, you "lock in" the deal before any
  other agency even knows the candidate exists.
